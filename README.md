# BLINDBOX FACTORY - A MOBILE TYCOON GAME

## Name
Blindbox Factory is a strategic management game where players delve into the exciting world of blindbox commerce, overseeing everything from factory production to the thrilling trade and collection of unique blindbox items.

## Description
Blindbox Factory is a tycoon game for children from 6-12, with simplified mechanics to ensure it's easy and fun for younger players to understand and enjoy. The core gameplay revolves around building and expanding a blindbox production factory, offering players an engaging and creative management experience.

Players start with a modest initial sum, enough to construct basic facilities like production lines and storage warehouses, all essential for commencing blindbox manufacturing. Once blindboxes are produced, players can choose to sell individual boxes retail for a steady income, or fulfill larger customer orders for higher profits. Earned money can be reinvested into expanding the factory, such as increasing its size or upgrading to more modern production lines. Additionally, players can use profits to purchase adorable mascots – special support characters that can accelerate production, reduce costs, or improve storage, making the factory operate more efficiently.

Throughout factory management, players need to pay attention to worker welfare to ensure their productivity. Furthermore, unexpected events will randomly occur in the game, like a rat-infested warehouse or a sudden power plant breakdown. Successfully resolving these events will reward players with additional resources, bonuses, or rare items, accelerating factory development.

All these efforts are geared towards the goal of building a large, visually appealing, and prominent Blindbox factory, complete with optimized production lines and splendidly decorated areas. This will help players earn as much money as possible, becoming a tycoon in the promising blindbox industry. The journey of factory development not only provides a sense of accomplishment but also sparks creativity and the joy of discovery for children, as they both manage and design their factory in their own unique style.
